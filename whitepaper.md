# $AI — Autistic Intellegenicum
## Concept whitepaper · Version 0.1

Status: concept stage. This document describes an artistic meme-coin concept and a fictional interactive archive. It does not announce a deployed token, offer a token for sale, or establish rights for holders.

## 01 / The signal and the noise

The internet has no shortage of opinions. Charts, headlines, group chats, and memes compete for the same finite resource: attention. $AI starts with a simple creative question: what if attention itself were an element?

Autistic Intellegenicum is the archive's fictional answer. It represents intense focus paired with a willingness to question patterns. The name is a creative identity, not a medical description, a claim about autistic people, or a measure of intelligence. The project makes no clinical claims.

The central narrative is “watch before you speak.” The meme is not that the compound can predict the market. It is that the compound keeps looking long after the timeline has moved on.

## 03 / How the lab works

The website makes the narrative interactive with three synthetic scenarios: sideways charts, timeline hype, and group chat panic. Each scenario has three possible written findings. A sample selects one of those findings at random.

A focus slider ranges from 20 to 100. The simulated focus result adds a random integer from 0 to 5 and caps the total at 100. Scenario noise starts at 12, 48, or 75 respectively. Reported noise is the scenario value multiplied by (1 − focus intensity / 125), rounded and floored at 1. These values are invented simulation parameters, not probabilities or investment signals.

The compound mixer adjusts the ratio of A to I. Applying a formula sets the lab focus intensity to 40 plus 0.6 times the A percentage, rounded. A balanced 50/50 formula therefore sets the focus intensity to 70. The mixer changes the simulation setting only.

The chart is generated from mathematical waves and a scenario-dependent trend. It uses no price feed. Visitors can pause its animation. The lab retains at most 20 recent samples in the browser's local storage and displays the latest five. Visitors can export the log as JSON or clear it. Storage may be unavailable in some browser configurations. The site has no wallet connection or trading action.
